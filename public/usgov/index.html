How It Works

1

We Scan

Sonde crawls your website with 30+ accessibility probes, analyzing every page against WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 requirements.

2

We Report

You receive a branded interactive report with a Sonde Score, detailed findings, WCAG criterion heatmap, and prioritized fix plans.

3

You Improve

Follow the fix plans, re-scan to track progress, and show measurable improvement before the compliance deadline.
