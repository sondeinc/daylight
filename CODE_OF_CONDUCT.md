# Code of Conduct

## Our Purpose

Daylight exists to improve government website accessibility for everyone.
The people most affected by inaccessible websites — people who use screen
readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, and other assistive technologies
— are the reason this project exists. Their experience and expertise are
invaluable, and this community must be a place where they can participate
safely and productively.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be constructive.** This project publishes findings about real websites
  run by real people. Critique the code, not the people behind it.
- **Be accurate.** If you report an issue, include specifics: finding IDs,
  URLs, WCAG criteria. Vague complaints help no one.
- **Be patient.** Government teams are often understaffed and under-resourced.
  The goal is to help them fix problems, not to embarrass them.
- **Respect lived experience.** If someone who uses assistive technology says
  a finding matters (or doesn't), listen.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments directed at individuals or
  organizations
- Doxxing or sharing private information about government employees
- Using findings or scores to publicly shame, threaten, or pressure
  individuals (as opposed to advocating for institutional improvement)
- Spam, promotional content, or off-topic solicitation
- Submitting intentionally false nominations or reports

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email
**community@sonde.life** with details. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours.
All reports are treated confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban participants who
violate this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions prioritize the safety
of community members who use assistive technologies.
